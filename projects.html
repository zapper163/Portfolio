REDACTOR EXPRÉS - A newspaper management simulator

Redactor Exprés is a game where you play as the editor-in-chief of a news outlet. Each day, you choose which stories to publish based on public interest. Once published, your earnings depend on how well you match those interests and can be used to acquire more compelling news, upgrade the newsroom, hire additional journalists, or secure better contracts.

This prototype was developed as a response to the Game Jam premise to create a casual game experience that encourages individuals—especially those who don’t typically engage with daily news—to feel more connected and curious about current events.

This game was developed in just one week for the Ludi Awards, organized by 3Cat and GameBCN.

FROST ENGINE - A videogame engine

Frost Engine is a video game engine that I have fully programmed myself. It is based on OpenGL and uses ImGui for the interface. The engine is capable of importing 3D models and can also manipulate them, including moving, scaling, rotating and assigning textures. The software also includes a GameObject hierarchy system, which allows the user to modify parameters and add components to the game object and its children. Additional features include an integrated audio subsystem using Wwise, with SoundSource and SoundListener components, SoundBank loading, and audio control.

Main Features

  • FBX & Texture loader
  • GameObject structure & Hierarchy
  • WWise audio Sub-System

NOrb - A pixelart 2D platformer

N'Orb is a 2D platform video game featuring isometric gameplay sections with pixel art. Embody, the protagonist, is a man who is obsessed with an idea, a thought, perfection, a goal. In his case, this is reflected as an orb of light. When this orb is kidnapped by a mysterious figure, the protagonist cannot allow it and sets out on his quest. Soon, his insecurities and fears began to manifest in his sleep. The game features timed 2D platforming sections, where the player has a limited time to reatch the end of the level, and issometric sections, where the nighmares appear in the form of combat, puzles or a mix of both.
